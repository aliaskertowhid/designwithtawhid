Case Study, Mobile Banking

BRAC Bank — Mobile Banking UX Redesign

BRAC Bank's app brings effortless, secure banking to your fingertips, this redesign focused on unifying accounts, simplifying transfers, and making the app feel modern and trustworthy.

Project Overview

One of Bangladesh's largest banks, reworked for everyday use.

BRAC Bank is a leading commercial bank in Bangladesh, serving millions of customers through its branch network and digital channels. Its mobile app sits at the center of that digital strategy, so this case study looks closely at the app's design, features, and overall experience — and what a redesign could do for it.

I rebuilt the app around a refreshed interface and a few key upgrades: unified account management, a simplified transfer flow, and personalized product recommendations, all aimed at boosting customer satisfaction and supporting the bank's broader digital transformation.

Before touching the interface, I spoke with existing app users and mapped the current account, transfer, and recommendation flows. I also went through App Store and Play Store reviews, along with BRAC Bank's Facebook page and post comments, to see where real users were getting stuck.

08
Key Takeaways

What this project reinforced.

01
Unify before you decorate

Consolidating accounts into one dashboard mattered more to users than any visual polish, it removed the biggest daily friction point first.

02
Shorten the path to money movement

Cutting the transfer flow down, with saved beneficiaries, was the single change users mentioned most in follow-up interviews.

03
Personalization needs real data

Recommendations only felt useful once they were grounded in actual spending patterns rather than generic product pushes.
