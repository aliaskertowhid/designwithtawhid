Case Study, Real Estate

RentFinder — Simplifying Property Rentals

A premium home rental app built for Bangladesh, designed to make finding a verified, trustworthy rental faster for students, professionals, and families.

Timeline
May – June 2023
Role

Industry

01
Project Overview

Renting a home, without the guesswork.

Finding a place to rent is usually slow and stressful. RentFinder set out to change that with a premium home rental app built specifically for Bangladesh.

Whether the user is a student, a young professional, or a family, RentFinder was designed to simplify the entire rental journey, from search through to move-in.

02
Research

Renters didn't trust what they saw online.

Interviews with renters across Dhaka and Sylhet surfaced the same complaint again and again: listing photos rarely matched reality, and there was no way to trust a property before visiting in person.

08
Key Takeaways

What this project reinforced.

01
Verification builds trust faster than polish

A simple "verified listing" badge, backed by real inspection, reduced drop-off far more than visual refinements did.

02
Combine filters, don't stack them

Letting users filter by commute time, budget, and amenities in one pass cut search sessions down noticeably.

03
Timelines and feedback loops matter

Finding the right interview candidates was harder than expected, but sticking to a self-imposed timeline and folding in user feedback along the way kept the project moving and sharpened the final design.
