03 — Background

The path wasn't a straight line.

Point A

The Frontlines

My career started in customer service and foundational IT support. Managing high-volume inquiries and troubleshooting complex systems taught me how to truly empathize with frustrated users and identify root-cause failures before they escalate.

The Pivot

Agency & Freelance Design

I transitioned into digital design, working remotely with agencies to refine platforms and revamp design systems. This phase was all about taking my operational empathy and translating it into scalable, accessible interfaces.

The Catalyst

Humber Polytechnic

I formalized my design education by earning a User Experience Design certificate. This experience sharpened my focus on complex architecture, usability testing, and enterprise-grade design systems.

Point B

Product Designer

Today, I am a Toronto-based Product Designer open to new opportunities. I am focused on building clean, dependable fintech and banking-adjacent products that solve real-world friction.
