Project Overview

Bringing native clarity to one of retail's most held cards.

The Walmart Rewards Mastercard is carried by millions of shoppers across North America as their primary credit vehicle for household groceries, general merchandise, and online purchases. Despite its widespread everyday use, account servicing remained tied to a legacy web browser portal.

My goal was to design an end-to-end native mobile application that empowers cardholders to track live balances, manage instalments directly from recent purchases, toggle on-demand security controls, and redeem rewards with complete clarity.

Responsibilities

User Research, Competitor Benchmarking, User Persona Development, Information Architecture, Wireframing, Design Token Architecture, High-Fidelity Prototyping, and Micro-interactions.

Target User

Everyday family shoppers, students, and budget-conscious individuals who use their Walmart Mastercard regularly and want seamless tracking without navigating browser portals.

03
Design Process

An iterative roadmap from discovery to delivery.

The redesign followed a user-centered design sprint cycle divided across three comprehensive phases: Discover & Define, Architecture & Wireframes, and High-Fidelity Design System delivery.

Phase 1 — Discovery & Research

Desk research, competitive app benchmarking, and qualitative user interview sessions to identify where retail cardholders drop off.

Phase 2 — Information Architecture

User journey mapping, task flow optimization, low-fidelity paper wireframes, and digital structural layouts.

Phase 3 — Visual System & Testing

Establishing Walmart's native design tokens, crafting high-contrast UI components, and validating usability across edge cases.

Phase 4 — End-to-End Production

Generating the 100+ production screen set covering onboarding, balance tracking, instalments, and security management.

04
Research

Uncovering friction in retail card management.

By reviewing customer complaint boards, retail banking forums, and existing card servicing ratings, clear patterns emerged: shoppers wanted quick confirmation that their transactions cleared, clarity on accumulated cash rewards, and zero confusion over payment due dates.

68% Drop-off Rate

Users reported frequently abandoning browser-based balance checks due to repeated multi-factor web prompts and session timeouts.

Reward Disconnect

Over 74% of cardholders did not know their exact reward dollar balance until prompted at physical self-checkout registers.

05
User Interview

Questions asked & what we heard.

I conducted 1-on-1 interview sessions with frequent Walmart shoppers holding the Mastercard to understand their monthly routines, budget tracking habits, and security concerns.

Questions Asked

• "How do you check your card balance before making large shopping trips?"
• "Have you ever tried to set up an instalment plan on a major purchase?"
• "How clear is it when and how your rewards can be applied to statements?"
• "What is the first thing you do if you misplace your card while shopping?"

What We Heard

• "I hate having to open Chrome on my phone just to see if my payment cleared."
• "I bought a TV last month and wanted to split it, but the website made it impossible."
• "Rewards feel hidden. I never know how much I've saved until the cashier asks."
• "If I lose my card, I don't want to wait on hold for 20 minutes to freeze it."

Competitive Analysis

Benchmarked against industry leaders.

I evaluated leading retail and banking applications to synthesize interaction models that customers already trust and expect.

Apple Card

Benchmark: Clear real-time cash back tracking and simplified interest calculators.
Takeaway: Plain-language statements eliminate payment anxiety.

Target Circle Card

Benchmark: Seamless integration between store perks and card accounts.
Takeaway: Surfacing rewards alongside recent store receipts drives high engagement.

Amazon Prime Card

Benchmark: Zero-effort point redemption directly applied to future orders.
Takeaway: Points must be actionable within two taps from the primary home dashboard.

Capital One Mobile

Benchmark: Instant on-demand card controls and virtual number provisioning.
Takeaway: Freeze/unfreeze must be accessible directly without diving into settings.

08
User Flow

Four essential journeys, mapped end to end.

Architecture maps were engineered to eliminate unnecessary friction and keep high-priority tasks reachable within seconds.

1. Instant Card Activation

Scan physical card with camera → SMS OTP confirmation → Set PIN → Auto-add to Apple/Google Wallet → Ready to spend.

2. Purchase-to-Instalment Flow

Transaction feed → Select eligible charge ($100+) → Choose 3, 6, or 12-month term → Review monthly fee → Confirm plan.

3. One-Tap Reward Redemption

Home dashboard → View live rewards points ($1 = 100 pts) → Select "Apply to Statement" or "Redeem in Store" → Success receipt.

4. Security & Card Controls

Account tab → Instant Card Freeze toggle → Lock international spend / online orders → Biometric confirmation.

11
Visual Design

The core screens that redefine the experience.

The final design focuses on five key functional areas that solve the historical friction points uncovered during research.

Home, in One Glance

High-contrast ledger display showing available credit, rewards balance, and next minimum payment with instant 1-tap "Pay Card" action.

Instalments, from Purchase to Plan

Converts any eligible purchase over $100 into transparent 3, 6, or 12-month instalments with zero hidden fees and clear monthly terms.

Controls Where People Expect Them

Direct access to card freeze, online transaction locks, international switches, and replacement requests without nested sub-menus.

Activation That Ends in a Working Card

Camera scan card recognition, frictionless SMS security verification, and automatic single-tap Apple Pay and Google Wallet provisioning.

Every Charge, Explained

No more vague statement line items. Every transaction features clear retailer branding, mapped location pins, categorized expense tagging, and a direct in-app dispute button.

Key Takeaways

What this project reinforced.

01
Retail integration is the true differentiator

Connecting physical in-store receipts and Walmart Pay directly to card servicing creates a cohesive ecosystem that standalone banking apps cannot match.

02
Friction kills daily financial utility

Eliminating web browser logins in favor of fast biometric authentication turned account checks into a frictionless, 5-second daily routine.

03
Instalment clarity builds long-term trust

Showing transparent dollar costs rather than complex APR interest percentages empowers users to budget responsibly without fee anxiety.

04
Design tokens enable real-world scale

Establishing strict semantic color and typographic tokens early on made expanding the application across 100+ screens seamless and mathematically consistent.
