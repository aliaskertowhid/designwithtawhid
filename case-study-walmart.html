Project Overview

Bringing native clarity to one of retail's most held cards.

The Walmart Rewards Mastercard is carried by millions of shoppers across North America as their primary credit card for groceries, household essentials, and online orders. Despite its massive daily volume, account management historically required logging into a third-party banking website through a mobile browser.

This case study designs a standalone, native mobile experience: unified balances, transparent instalment plans, instant on-demand security controls, and rewards made spendable at checkout.

Responsibilities

End-to-End Product Design, User Research, Qualitative Interviews, Information Architecture, Wireframing, Design Tokens, Interactive Figma Prototyping, and Production Handoff.

Target User

Budget-conscious households, frequent in-store Walmart shoppers, and students seeking instant clarity over balances, payment dates, and rewards without web portal friction.

Research

Finding the breaking points of retail card servicing.

By auditing credit card community boards, store customer feedback, and App Store reviews for competing retail store cards, three major friction categories emerged: verification drop-offs, reward ambiguity, and opaque payment posting times.

Desk research & cardholder community feedback analysis walmart-research-insights.png
05
User Interview

Questions asked & what we heard.

I engaged active cardholders in guided interviews focusing on their weekly routines, payment habits, and pain points when making unplanned larger purchases.

Questions Asked

• "How do you check your card balance before making large shopping trips?"
• "Have you ever tried to set up an instalment plan on a major purchase?"
• "How clear is it when and how your rewards can be applied to statements?"
• "What is the first thing you do if you misplace your card while shopping?"

What We Heard

• "I hate having to open Chrome on my phone just to see if my payment cleared."
• "I bought a TV last month and wanted to split it, but the website made it impossible."
• "Rewards feel hidden. I never know how much I've saved until the cashier asks."
• "If I lose my card, I don't want to wait on hold for 20 minutes to freeze it."

07
Competitive Analysis

Benchmarked against industry leaders.

Evaluated leading retail cards (Apple Card, Target Circle, Amazon Prime Card, Capital One) to identify UX benchmarks across rewards visibility, instalment clarity, and on-demand controls.

Apple Card & Capital One

Benchmark: Real-time category tracking, plain-language interest terms, and instant biometric authorization.
Opportunity: Translate transparent balance reporting into a dedicated retail card experience.

Target Circle & Amazon Prime

Benchmark: Zero-effort automatic discounts and one-click rewards redemption directly at points of purchase.
Opportunity: Connect in-store Walmart receipts with digital statement rewards credit.

11
Visual Design

The core screens that redefine the card experience.

The core features designed to solve the primary friction points uncovered in research, presented in full production fidelity.

Home, in one glance

A unified financial card showing real-time balance ($2,317.42), available credit, cash reward totals, payment due dates, and one-tap payment scheduling without navigating menus.

Instalments, from purchase to plan

Directly convert qualifying retail purchases ($100+) into 3, 6, or 12-month instalment plans with transparent dollar fees, clear monthly commitments, and zero hidden penalties.

Controls where people expect them

Instant card freeze/unfreeze toggle, online transaction locking, international use switches, and replacement card ordering accessible in one tap.

Activation that ends in a working card

Frictionless optical camera scanning of the physical card, SMS two-factor verification, and instant auto-provisioning to Apple Wallet and Google Pay.

Every charge, explained

Eliminates cryptic statement lines. Each transaction displays verified merchant logos, mapped store locations, itemized receipt histories, and an in-app dispute button.

13
Key Takeaways

What this project reinforced.

01
Retail integration is the true differentiator

Connecting physical in-store receipts and Walmart Pay directly to card servicing creates a cohesive ecosystem that standalone banking apps cannot match.

02
Friction kills daily financial utility

Eliminating web browser logins in favor of fast biometric authentication turned account checks into a frictionless, 5-second daily routine.

03
Instalment clarity builds long-term trust

Showing transparent dollar costs rather than complex APR interest percentages empowers users to budget responsibly without fee anxiety.

04
Design tokens enable real-world scale

Establishing strict semantic color and typographic tokens early on made expanding the application across 100+ screens seamless and mathematically consistent.
