03 — Background

The path wasn't a straight line.

Point A
2013 · Sylhet

First Lines

It started with volunteer graphic design work — flyers, banners, logos, business cards — turned around fast for a small local shop. Nothing formal, no design theory yet, just a first instinct for making things that had to communicate something under a deadline.

The Foundation
2018–2021 · Toronto

Learning How Things Work

A Computer Systems Technician diploma, a Google IT Support certificate, and NPower Canada's Junior IT Analyst program — operating systems, networking, troubleshooting, Agile fundamentals. Before I ever touched a design tool, I learned how the systems underneath actually behave.

The Leap
Jan 2021 – Present · Worldwide

Going Independent

Started taking on freelance UX/UI work — websites, landing pages, research, wireframes — for clients ranging from individuals to small companies. It's stayed a constant thread running underneath everything since.

The Apprenticeship
Apr–Oct 2022 · Sylhet

Learning From Someone Else's Process

A UX internship at Twinkle Creative — assisting with research and usability testing on a mobile app, building wireframes and prototypes in Figma alongside senior designers, and getting a first real look at how a design team actually operates.

The First Seat
Nov 2022 – May 2024 · Sylhet

First Full-Time Role

Moved into a full-time UX/UI Designer role at Twinkle Creative, redesigning their design system and working directly with product leads — real ownership over interfaces, accessibility, and how design handed off to development.

The Present Chapter
Mar 2024 – Present · Toronto (Remote)

Where I Am Now

UX/UI Designer at Aero Design Agency, refining platform features and design systems remotely — continuing to sharpen the same instincts: systems thinking, accessibility, and interfaces that hold up under real use.

Point B
2025 – Present · Toronto

Formalizing the Craft

Currently completing a UX Design graduate certificate at Humber Polytechnic — bringing everything before it (the systems thinking, the operational grounding, the years of real client work) into one deliberate, formal design practice.
