03 — Background

More than a design-tool user.

I'm a Toronto-based Product Designer, trained at Humber Polytechnic, focused on fintech and banking-adjacent products — dashboards, statements, and account flows that need to feel dependable, not just polished.

My path into design ran through administrative operations, IT support, and customer-facing service roles — work that meant untangling real operational friction before I ever opened a design tool. That shows up in how I design now: systems thinking, edge cases, and what happens when something goes wrong.

Chapter 01

The Technical Base

Google IT Support Professional — Mastered the fundamentals of operating systems, networking, and system security to understand how complex architecture functions.

Chapter 02

The Human Element

Security Operations & Mobile Patrol — Managed physical operations across retail and surveillance, developing a hyper-awareness of how people behave under friction and stress.

Chapter 03

The Digital Craft

UX Design Certification (2025) — Graduated from Humber Polytechnic, learning how to translate that operational logic and human empathy into clean, accessible interfaces.
